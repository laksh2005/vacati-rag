# Booking FAQ

## How do I pay?

Vacati takes Visa, Mastercard, and American Express, plus SEPA bank transfer for bookings over EUR 3,000. A 30% deposit is charged at confirmation and the balance is charged automatically 30 days before check-in.

## Which currency am I charged in?

All bookings are priced and charged in euros. If your card is denominated in another currency, your bank sets the conversion rate and may add a foreign transaction fee. Vacati does not add a currency conversion charge.

## Can I add guests after booking?

Yes, up to the property's stated maximum occupancy. Extra guests beyond the rate's included occupancy are charged at EUR 45 per person per night. Exceeding the stated maximum occupancy is grounds for cancellation without refund.

## What is included in the nightly rate?

The nightly rate includes accommodation, utilities, Wi-Fi, the stated housekeeping schedule, and linen and towels. It does not include the local tourist tax, which is collected by the property at EUR 4 per person per night for guests over 12, capped at 7 nights.

## What if something is wrong on arrival?

Report the issue to the property manager first, then to Vacati support within 24 hours of check-in. Issues reported inside that window are eligible for a remedy or partial refund; issues reported later generally are not.

## How do I contact support?

Support is reachable by email and in-app chat from 08:00 to 22:00 CET, seven days a week. Urgent in-stay issues have a 24-hour emergency line listed in the check-in pack.
