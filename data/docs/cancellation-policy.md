# Cancellation and Refund Policy

Applies to all stays booked directly through Vacati. Property-specific windows override the general rules below where they differ.

## General cancellation windows

Guests may cancel any direct booking free of charge within 24 hours of confirmation, provided check-in is more than 7 days away. After that grace period the property-specific window applies.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7-10 business days. Vacati service fees are refunded only when the cancellation falls in a fully refundable window.

## Villa Aurora

Villa Aurora is a peak-demand property and uses a stricter window than the Vacati default.

Cancellations made **more than 30 days** before check-in receive a full refund of the accommodation total. Cancellations made **between 14 and 30 days** before check-in receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made **less than 14 days** before check-in are non-refundable, and the first night is charged as a no-show fee if the guest does not arrive.

Date changes at Villa Aurora are permitted once, free of charge, up to 30 days before check-in, subject to availability.

## Cliff House

Cliff House uses a more flexible window than Villa Aurora.

Cancellations made **more than 14 days** before check-in receive a full refund of the accommodation total. Cancellations made **between 48 hours and 14 days** before check-in receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made **less than 48 hours** before check-in are non-refundable.

Date changes at Cliff House are permitted twice, free of charge, up to 7 days before check-in, subject to availability.

## Weather and force majeure

If a government authority issues an evacuation order or travel ban covering the destination during the booked dates, the stay is fully refundable regardless of the property window. Rain, heat, and ordinary poor weather are not grounds for a policy exception.

## Group bookings

Bookings of four or more rooms are treated as group bookings. Group bookings require a 30% deposit at confirmation, and that deposit is non-refundable inside 45 days of check-in.
