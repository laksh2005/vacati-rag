# Cliff House

A three-bedroom modern house above Praiano, sleeping six guests.

## The property

Cliff House has three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a heated plunge pool, and a wide sea-facing deck. Housekeeping is twice weekly. Air conditioning is fitted in every room.

Wi-Fi is 120 Mbps. There is a dedicated desk and monitor in the second bedroom, which makes the house workable for remote-working guests.

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 15:00. Check-out is by 11:00. Self check-in via a keypad is available, so late arrivals do not need to coordinate with the property manager.

## Rates and minimum stay

Peak season runs 1 June to 15 September. The peak nightly rate is EUR 890 and the minimum stay is 5 nights. Shoulder season covers April, May, and October at EUR 610 per night with a 3-night minimum. The house is open year-round.

A refundable damage deposit of EUR 500 is held against the card on file and released within 14 days of check-out.

## Access

Cliff House has parking for one car at the door and step-free access from the parking space to the main living level. Two of the three bedrooms are up a flight of stairs.

## House rules

No events or parties. Quiet hours are 22:00 to 08:00. One dog under 15 kg is permitted with prior approval and a EUR 80 cleaning surcharge. Smoking is not permitted anywhere on the property.
