# Villa Aurora

A four-bedroom clifftop villa on the south coast of Amalfi, sleeping eight guests.

## The property

Villa Aurora has four ensuite bedrooms, an infinity pool facing the sea, an outdoor kitchen, and a terraced garden. The villa is staffed daily by a housekeeper and includes a private chef on request at additional cost.

Wi-Fi throughout is fibre-backed at 300 Mbps. There is no air conditioning in the two garden-level bedrooms, which stay naturally cool.

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 16:00. Check-out is by 10:00. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and are arranged with the property manager, not through Vacati support.

## Rates and minimum stay

Peak season runs 1 June to 15 September. The peak nightly rate is EUR 1,450 and the minimum stay is 7 nights. Shoulder season covers April, May, and late September at EUR 980 per night with a 4-night minimum. The villa closes from November to March.

A refundable damage deposit of EUR 1,000 is held against the card on file and released within 14 days of check-out.

## Access

Villa Aurora is reached by 62 stone steps from the road. There is no vehicle access to the door and no lift, so the property is not suitable for guests with limited mobility. Luggage porterage is included on arrival and departure.

## House rules

No events or parties. Quiet hours are 23:00 to 07:00. Pets are not permitted. Smoking is not permitted indoors.
