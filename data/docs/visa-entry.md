# Visa and Entry Requirements

Vacati does not provide immigration advice. The notes below are general and guests are responsible for verifying requirements with the relevant consulate before travel.

## Schengen area

Italy is in the Schengen area. Travellers holding passports from the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, and most of Latin America may enter for tourism without a visa for up to 90 days in any rolling 180-day period.

Passports must be valid for at least three months beyond the intended date of departure from the Schengen area, and must have been issued within the previous ten years.

## ETIAS

From the date ETIAS enters into force, visa-exempt travellers will need an approved ETIAS travel authorisation before boarding. The application is completed online, costs EUR 7, and is valid for three years or until the passport expires. Applicants under 18 or over 70 are exempt from the fee.

## Travellers who need a visa

Nationals of countries outside the visa-exempt list must apply for a Schengen short-stay (Type C) visa at the Italian consulate covering their place of residence. Applications open no earlier than six months before travel and should be filed at least 15 working days ahead. Consulates typically require proof of accommodation, return travel, travel insurance covering EUR 30,000 in medical costs, and evidence of sufficient funds.

Vacati can issue a booking confirmation letter for a visa application on request. Contact support at least 21 days before the appointment.

## Insurance

Travel insurance is not mandatory for visa-exempt visitors but is strongly recommended, and it is mandatory as part of a Type C visa application.
